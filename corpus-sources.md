# Corpus sources — how to get the 53 items

The corpus behind this study is **not in this repository**. Its 53 items are
copyrighted primary sources — Shannon's papers, theses, talks, and interviews
— plus the secondary books, profiles, and obituaries used for context and
triangulation. Distributing them would be copyright infringement. Instead,
this file lists every item's public source so anyone can rebuild the corpus
themselves.

Built November 2026. Source cutoff November 25, 2026. Items: 53 total — 16
primary, 37 secondary. Of the primary items, 11 were read in full or in
large part, 2 in part, and 3 could not be read (see below).

## Method

1. Download each item from its source URL below.
2. Extract text. HTML pages: save the article text. PDFs: extract with a tool
   like `pdftotext`; the image-only items need OCR first (e.g. `ocrmypdf` or
   Tesseract) — they are marked `image-only` below.
3. Keep the manifest fields per item: collection, year, source URL, format,
   grade, and status. Do not renumber or silently substitute items.
4. Read chronologically in bounded tranches rather than searching only for
   known phrases, and record principles, changes across decades, boundary
   conditions, and disconfirming evidence as you go.
5. Grade every item A–D by provenance (see `methodology-and-corpus.md`).
   Grade A or clean Grade B is required for a load-bearing exact quotation.
   Grade C is paraphrase unless independently confirmed. Grade D is discovery
   only.
