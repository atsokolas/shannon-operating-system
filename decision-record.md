# Decision record

Copy this file for each consequential decision. Fill it in **before** the
outcome is known, then review it on the review date. See
`operating-framework.md` for the full 10-step sequence this template comes from.

```text
Decision:
Deadline / owner:
Reversibility:

Selection set (what is being chosen, from what):
Uncertainty (what is unknown; what would reduce it most):
Level: technical / semantic / effectiveness

Fitting algebra / model:
Limit (what is possible in principle; what is impossible):
What the analysis withholds (existence vs. construction; delay; cost):
Smallest version built (or: why none):
Redundancy priced (margin; unicity distance):

Genealogy (debts; priority claims checked):
Strongest opposing case:
What would change my mind:

Decision and size:
Conditions / tripwires:
Review date:
Confidence range:
```

## Dossier variant (for a maintained judgment — an investment, a product call, a hire)

Keep one record per judgment and update it when evidence changes, instead of
rewriting the thesis:

```text
Current claim
Confidence and evidence quality
Attached sources
What changed
What strengthened
What weakened
Open questions
Decision / next move
Review trigger
```

## Review checklist

- Separate decision quality from outcome quality. Noise is real: a good
  decision can have a bad outcome, and a bad decision a good one.
- Identify skill, luck, missing knowledge, and broken process.
- State what the analysis withheld, and whether the withholding mattered.
- Track repeated omissions and repeated rationalizations.
- Preserve what was known at the time; do not rewrite the original thesis.
- When a claim rests on a reconstruction, say so. When a quotation is
  second-hand, say so. When a source could not be read, say so.
